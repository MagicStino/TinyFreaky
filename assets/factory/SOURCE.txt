Factory data source: official Arturia MIDI Control Center 1.23.0.134 Windows distribution
https://dl.arturia.net/products/mccu/soft/MIDI_Control_Center__1_23_0_134.exe
Downloaded 2026-10-15. Installer was extracted, not executed.

Original factory banks are preserved byte-for-byte in original-factory-banks.zip.
Original MCC sample and wavetable files are preserved in original-sample-wavetable-assets.zip.
Bank filenames and metadata are retained. Duplicate decoded payloads are indexed
once in the instrument while all original bank locations remain in index.json.

Factory data belongs to its respective authors; the plugin's MIT code license
does not relicense these assets. All conversions are explicitly partial.
