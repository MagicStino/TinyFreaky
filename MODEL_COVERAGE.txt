Model coverage, 0.3 preview

Native IDs 0-15 remain stable.
0 Basic Waves / 1 SuperWave: independent oscillator implementations.
2 Wavetable: Mutable Instruments Plaits WavetableEngine.
3 Harmo: Mutable Instruments Plaits AdditiveEngine.
4 Karplus Strong: Mutable Instruments Plaits StringEngine.
5 Virtual Analog: Mutable Instruments Plaits VirtualAnalogEngine.
6 Waveshaper: Mutable Instruments Plaits WaveshapingEngine.
7 Two Operator FM: Mutable Instruments Plaits FMEngine.
8 Formant: Mutable Instruments Plaits GrainEngine (formant oscillator).
9 Chords: Mutable Instruments Plaits ChordEngine.
10 Speech: Mutable Instruments Plaits SpeechEngine, including word-bank resources.
11 Modal: Mutable Instruments Plaits ModalEngine.
12 Noise: Mutable Instruments Plaits NoiseEngine.
13 Bass / 14 SawX / 15 Harm: independent approximations.

These source cores are functional oscillator paths, not proof of equality with Arturia's adaptations or its macro response curves. No proprietary firmware implementation is included.

User wavetable, Sample, Scan/Cloud/Hit granular and input Vocoder paths remain unavailable. Original sample/wavetable files are preserved, but bundling an asset is not an implementation of its engine. Original binary factory sequences/modulation lanes are preserved, not decoded. The new 64-step sequencer is an independent editable note sequence.
