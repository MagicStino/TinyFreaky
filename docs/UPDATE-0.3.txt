Tinyfreaky 0.3 preview

A shaded green chassis and glossy inset panels keep the orange controls and large labels readable. The Force interface keeps six top-level tabs. MATRIX shows all 35 routes; use its Q-Link banks for sources and ASSIGNMENTS for the three configurable destinations. Zero-depth cells are dim; nonzero cells are orange. Filter Amount and Envelope > Cutoff are the same bipolar route, fixing the previous extra envelope contribution.

RHYTHM has ARPEGGIATOR, SEQUENCER and eight note-editor banks (STEPS 1-8 through STEPS 57-64). Enable ARP and hold notes; choose Up, Order, Random or a repeating Pattern. Rate includes straight and triplet divisions; swing ranges from 50% (straight) to 75%. Octaves, gate and Hold are available. For sequences, choose PLAY and edit pitches or REST in the step banks. Length is 1-64, with gate, velocity, transpose and restart. Internal clock follows Tempo; Host clock waits for host playback and follows its tempo. The display reports the current step. The desktop editor has corresponding rhythm/setup/note pages.

Eleven oscillator paths now call original MIT-licensed Mutable Instruments cores, including the Speech word bank. Pinning, attribution and unmodified upstream source are in third_party/eurorack. These are not a claim that other implementations are identical. Basic Waves, SuperWave, Bass, SawX and Harm remain independent approximations.

The 448-entry factory archive is retained. 334 entries use available oscillator models; 114 require user-table/sample/granular/vocoder support and remain explicitly unavailable/muted. Identified factory arp mode, range, division, swing and enable settings are now imported. Original binary sequence/modulation tracks are preserved but not decoded. Unavailable factory sequences are not replaced with the editor's demo pattern. LFO/time/filter response curves and original sound equivalence remain unverified.

MFZ2 stores all 148 numeric controls, including the 64 sequence notes. MFZ1 patches with the original 70 controls load with defaults for the new controls. Desktop MFV3 states accept either 70 or 148 controls. Numeric parameter IDs already present in the previous Force/desktop plugin are retained. The corrected Filter Amount alias can change the tone of older saved patches that relied on the previous extra envelope contribution.

Validation: engine/state/factory/rhythm tests under ASan/UBSan, Force host ABI tests, all generated Force page bounds and touch layout checks, ARM hard-float ELF checks, Linux desktop Steinberg validator and host smoke test. Exact factory sound and physical Force CPU/touchscreen behaviour still require device testing. Windows/macOS of this revision require CI validation. No new missing engine is advertised as implemented.
