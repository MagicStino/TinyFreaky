TinyFreaky 0.3.8

Corrected decay/release taper: 25% now gives 200 ms instead of 20 ms, while zero retains the 2 ms transient setting. Attack timing is unchanged. The UI reports actual envelope times. Envelope coefficients are calculated once per sample rather than once per voice.

The filtered-noise adapter no longer spends half its clock control range at sub-audible rates, and its internal filter center has been raised two octaves. Factory noise cutoff calibration respects low-pass, band-pass and high-pass routing, rather than stacking incompatible filters.

Factory calibration extends accidentally truncated plucks and very short string/modal excitation. Patch names and slots stay stable. Existing saved user parameter values are retained, but the corrected shared decay/noise playback will sound different.

The supported factory bank is audited across C3/C4/C5, and the CI sound regression checks every one of its 342 playable slots for a usable audible body. The 114 unsupported slots remain explicitly unavailable.
